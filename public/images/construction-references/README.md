All are 1536 × 1024 PNGs, with a single A, front-left perspective, navy backdrop and warm-white illumination.
They illustrate construction appearance; they do not certify dimensions, fastening, electrical design, production details or calibrated light output.
Small generation differences in camera, letter shape and wall texture are acceptable for reference imagery; these are not exact CAD renders.

## Intended use
Use these in the illuminated construction chooser and optional enlarged reference view. Preserve the existing native SVG references for flat acrylic, metal, ACP, vinyl and excluded types. Keep live anatomy diagrams driven by the actual project; do not replace dimensioned diagrams with generated pictures.
Display at a consistent 3:2 ratio with object-fit: contain; do not crop off the silhouette or halo.
Supply adjacent construction labels and alt text. Lazy-load chooser images, and keep the existing SVG reference as a load-error fallback.
No application UI consuming these assets was changed during generation/planning.
